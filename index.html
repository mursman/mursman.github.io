About

One person, two crafts

I'm Murshedur Rahman — the person behind the mursman name — a graphics designer and digital painter with 9+ years in Photoshop and Illustrator. From February 2020 to July 2021 I worked as a portrait designer at Doggovinci, creating Renaissance oil-painting-style pet portraits and themed template designs in Photoshop for thousands of customers. Since 2021 I've been a Template Creator and Pet Portrait Designer at Nextgenpaws.com, and today I also work as an Image Annotator at RYU Data Center, alongside the freelance design work I've done since 2017.

In parallel I design, build and ship my own apps — FajrAlarm, Neervia, VectorDrawableX and Clean Frame — owning everything from the first sketch to the store listing. I don't write the code myself — I design the product and its UI/UX, write the specs, direct AI tools that do the engineering, debug and fix issues, and test everything on real devices. Portrait or product, the standard is the same: real work, delivered properly.
